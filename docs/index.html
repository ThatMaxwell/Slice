FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Slice free?

Yes. The built-in AI runs Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite through Puter.js at no cost to you (you sign in to Puter once). You can also paste your own Gemini key, or any OpenAI-compatible key, in Settings.

What does Slice remember about me?

Your searches help it learn your taste and how you talk — stored locally on your PC. Personal searches (health, money, relationships, identity, contact details) are filtered out before they're stored, and the AI never sees them. Throwaway searches like “yo” are discarded. You can view and delete everything in Settings → Memory.

Is it really Chromium? Do my sites work?

Slice renders pages with Blink, the same engine as Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi — so sites work the way you expect. Google's background services (sync, Safe Browsing pings, component updater, Privacy Sandbox ad topics) are switched off.

Does uBlock Origin come built in?

Slice ships uBlock Origin's own filter lists, compiled into a native blocking engine and switched on by default — network blocking, cosmetic filtering and scriptlets. Toggle it any time from the shield.

Can I use it without the AI?

Totally. The top bar is a plain search box for your chosen engine. Set web search to “off”, turn off the results panel, and Slice is just a fast, clean browser.

Mac, Linux, Android?

Windows is first. Android is coming soon. Slice is built on cross-platform tech, so Mac and Linux builds are on the roadmap too.
