FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Slice free?

Yes. The built-in AI runs Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite through Puter.js at no cost to you. Puter is the only account Slice asks for, and you sign in once during setup. You can also paste your own key for Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq or OpenRouter, or run local models with Ollama or LM Studio.

My Gemini key starts with AQ. — does it work?

Yes. Google now hands out “auth keys” that start with AQ. instead of the older AIza… keys. Slice sends both to Gemini's native API, and works out which auth style a key needs. Paste it in Settings → AI and hit “Test connection”.

What does Slice remember about me?

Your searches help it learn your taste and how you talk. That's stored on your PC. Personal searches (health, money, relationships, identity, contact details) are filtered out before they're stored, and the AI never sees them. Throwaway searches like “yo” are discarded. You can see and delete everything in Settings → Memory.

Is it really Chromium? Do my sites work?

Slice renders pages with Blink, the same engine as Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi, so sites work the way you expect. Google's background services are switched off.

Does uBlock Origin come built in?

Slice ships uBlock Origin's own filter lists, compiled into a native blocking engine and switched on by default. It handles network blocking, cosmetic filtering and scriptlets. Toggle it any time from the shield.

Mac, Linux, Android?

Windows comes first. Android is coming soon. Mac and Linux builds are on the roadmap.
